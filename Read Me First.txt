==========================================================
  HAND MOUSE
  Control your computer with your hands
==========================================================

Hand Mouse uses your webcam to follow your hands. Move the
mouse, click, scroll, zoom, present slides with a laser
pointer, control Teams, and more. Works with one screen or
several.

  - Nothing to install
  - Works offline
  - Private: your camera video is never saved or sent
    anywhere

You need: Windows 10 or 11, and a webcam.


==========================================================
  GET STARTED
==========================================================

1. UNZIP
   Right-click HandMouse.zip > "Extract All" > "Extract".
   (It won't work from inside the zip.)

2. ALLOW THE CAMERA
   Settings (Windows key + I) > Privacy & security > Camera
   Turn ON "Camera access" and
   "Let desktop apps access your camera".
   Close other camera apps (Zoom, Teams video, etc.).

3. START IT
   Double-click HandMouse.exe
   - "Windows protected your PC"? Click "More info" >
     "Run anyway".
   - WAIT up to 30 seconds. Don't click it again.
   - A small camera window opens. You're ready!

   Keep the black window open. Closing it stops Hand Mouse.


==========================================================
  TRY IT
==========================================================

Sit an arm's length from the camera with light on your
hands. Keep your hand inside the PURPLE BOX.

With ONE hand:
  1. MOVE ......... Open hand, move slowly
  2. CLICK ........ Tap thumb + index finger together
  3. SCROLL ....... Index + middle finger up together,
                    move hand up or down
  4. PAUSE ........ Make a fist
  5. VOLUME ....... Thumbs up and hold

With TWO hands (sit back a little so both fit):
  Your right hand moves the mouse. Your left hand does
  commands and clicks.

  6. STEADY CLICK . Aim with your right hand, tap thumb +
                    index on your LEFT hand
  7. ZOOM ......... Pinch both hands (left first), then
                    move them apart or together
  8. ON / OFF ..... Make two fists and hold for 1 second

Left-handed? Click the camera window and press H.


==========================================================
  PRESENTING SLIDES
==========================================================

Start a PowerPoint slide show or present in Google Slides
(full screen). Hand Mouse switches to presentation mode by
itself. The camera window turns orange: PRESENTING.

  Swipe open hand left ...... Next slide
  Swipe open hand right ..... Previous slide
  Thumbs up / down (hold) ... Next / previous slide
  Point index finger ........ Laser pointer
  Point, then pinch ......... Draw on the slide

In a Teams meeting or anywhere else: press Ctrl+Alt+P to
turn presentation mode on or off.

Presenting across a conference room? Start with
"Hand Mouse Far Mode.bat" instead of HandMouse.exe.
Good lighting on your hands matters most.


==========================================================
  APPS WITH SPECIAL GESTURES
==========================================================

Gestures change automatically for: PowerPoint, Google
Slides, Teams, Zoom, YouTube, Spotify and SolidWorks.
The camera window shows which app is active ("App: ...").

See "Gesture Cheat Sheet.txt" for every gesture.


==========================================================
  HOW TO STOP
==========================================================

  - Click the camera window, then press Q
  - Or close the black window
  - Ctrl+Alt+H pauses Hand Mouse from any app
  - Emergency stop: move your real mouse into a corner
    of the screen


==========================================================
  PROBLEMS?
==========================================================

Nothing happens .......... Wait 30 seconds. Check your
                           antivirus didn't block it.
Camera black / won't open  Close other camera apps.
                           Check step 2.
"no hand" ................ More light. Palm to camera.
Shaky pointer ............ More light. Move slower.
"REAL MOUSE - paused" .... Take your hand off your mouse.
Keys don't work .......... Click the camera window first.
Only one hand detected ... Sit farther back, keep hands
                           apart.
Swipes don't change slides Watch the green bar while you
                           swipe; move sideways about
                           6 inches. Or use thumbs up/down.
Only one screen used ..... Settings > System > Display >
                           "Extend these displays".
VCRUNTIME140.dll message . Install "Microsoft Visual C++
                           Redistributable" (x64) from
                           Microsoft.
Anything else ............ Send a screenshot of the black
                           window to whoever shared this.


==========================================================
  CHANGING GESTURES (optional)
==========================================================

Gestures and app settings are in hand_mouse_settings.json.
Open it with Notepad, edit, save, and restart Hand Mouse.
If you break it, delete the file and a fresh one is made.
